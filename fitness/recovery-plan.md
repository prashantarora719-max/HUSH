# Recovery plan (recovery-coach) — v1

- **Sleep:** you average ~7 h; aim 7.5-8 h on training nights, fixed wake time. Short sleep in a deficit shifts loss from fat toward lean mass (Nedeltcheva 2010).
- **Readiness score (daily, 1-5)** in the tracker: sleep, soreness, mood, hunger, motivation. 2+ days ≤2 or lifts down in 2 sessions ⇒ cut sets 30-40% for a week. Deload: weeks 6 and 12, or on trigger.
- **Stress:** 5/10 (moderate, desk-based sales job). Plan unchanged; if stress or sleep worsens (readiness ≤2 for 2 days) volume drops first. Training ends ~9 pm — keep caffeine before 2 pm and a 30-min wind-down before a ~11:30 pm bedtime so late sessions don't cut sleep.
- **Vitamin D:** low; follow the prescribed 60,000 IU weekly protocol, re-test after ~8 weeks (low D affects recovery and mood).
- **Caffeine:** none after ~2 pm; alcohol minimal in the deficit (sleep, appetite, calories).
- **Skin:** shower soon after sweaty sessions, clean pillowcase/towel weekly, keep sleep consistent; see a dermatologist for seb-derm treatment.
- **Authority:** the recovery coach may cap volume, steps or the deficit when readiness data is poor.
