# Training plan (hypertrophy-coach) — v1

**Assessment.** ~2 years consistent lifting, PPL 6 days (completes ~5), sessions 90-120 min. Loads (bench 60-70 kg x8-10, incline DB 30 kg x8, squat 60-70 kg x8-10, leg press 200-240 kg x8, RDL ~70 kg x8-10, pulldown 67 kg x8-10) = early intermediate: still able to make progress, so muscle retention (and some gain) is realistic while cutting.

**Change from current:** 5 sessions/week (Push-Pull-Legs-Upper-Lower; Thu/Sun rest), ≤75 min per session. Reasons: deficit reduces recovery capacity (Murphy & Koehler 2022), 10-20 hard sets/muscle/wk is enough (Schoenfeld 2017), and 2x/week frequency per muscle is covered. Keep load heavy; trim junk volume, not intensity.

**Weekly hard sets (approx):** chest 9-12, back 14, side delts 8-12, biceps 8, triceps 8, quads 9, hamstrings 8, calves 7-10.

**Progression:** double progression within rep ranges; RIR 2→1 across a 5-week block; deload weeks 6 and 12 (~60% sets, -10% load). Details per session and per week are in `tracker.xlsx` → *Training Plan* / *Training Log*.

**Lat pulldown stack is capped at 67 kg:** use slow eccentrics, pauses, higher reps, single-arm work or machine/assisted pull-ups.
