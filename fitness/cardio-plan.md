# Cardio & steps (cardio-coach) — v1

You already walk 60-105 min/day on the walkpad, which likely puts you near 9-12k steps (UNMEASURED: log steps for Week 1 — this sets the real baseline).

- **Weeks 1-2:** keep the current walkpad routine (morning 30-45 min + evening 15-20 min); floor 10,000 steps/day. No new cardio.
- **Lever, not default:** if loss stalls (<0.3%/wk for 2 weeks, adherence ≥85%), add +1,000 steps/day (e.g. +10 min evening walk) OR cut 150 kcal — one lever only.
- **Cap:** 14,000 steps/day. Beyond that, returns are small and leg-day recovery suffers.
- **No HIIT.** Not needed at this deficit, and it competes with leg recovery. Optional: 1 x 20-30 min easy bike/incline walk on rest days if steps drop.
- Schedule around lifting: do the walkpad after lifting, never instead of it. Walks right after meals help appetite and glucose.
Evidence: NEAT/steps are the lowest-fatigue way to widen a deficit (Levine; Hall); expect conservative kcal estimates (±30%).
