Why Hush

Dictation apps got fast.
Then they got nosy.

Most modern dictation tools stream your voice to cloud servers, charge a monthly fee, and quietly rewrite what you said. Hush takes the opposite path.

🔒

Nothing leaves your Mac

The speech model runs on your own Apple Silicon chip. No audio uploads, no screenshots of your screen, no account. Dictate contracts, client notes, or your diary with a clear conscience.

✈️

Works offline, anywhere

On a flight, in a basement, on hotel Wi-Fi that barely loads a page — Hush doesn't care. After a one-time setup download, it never needs the internet again.

You control the edit

How it works

Three motions. That's the whole app.

one

Hold

Press and hold right ⌥ Option in any app — Mail, WhatsApp, Notion, your code editor. A red dot appears in your menu bar.

two

Speak

Talk the way you actually talk. Ums, pauses, "no wait, Friday" corrections, English, Hindi, or both mixed together — all fine.

three

Release

Let go of the key. One to two seconds later, clean text lands exactly where your cursor was. Your clipboard is untouched.

your voice→ speech model on your chip→ cleanup, your level→ text at your cursor

The amber steps run entirely on your Mac. There is no server step — that's the point.

Getting started

Set up once, about 15 minutes

Most of it is a one-time model download. A full guide ships inside the download.

  • 1

    Download and unzip

    Grab the zip below and move the hush folder somewhere permanent, like your home folder.

  • 2

    Run setup

    Right-click setup.command and choose Open. It installs everything and downloads the 1.6 GB speech model — the part that makes Hush private and offline.

  • 3

    Grant three permissions

    In System Settings > Privacy & Security, allow Terminal for Microphone, Input Monitoring, and Accessibility — the same permissions every dictation tool needs.

  • 4

    Start talking

    Double-click Start Hush.command, wait for the Ready notification, click into any text field, and hold the key.

Questions

The short answers

Does it really work with no internet?

Yes. After the one-time setup download, every dictation runs on your Mac's own chip. Airplane mode is a fine place to write.

Which languages does it support?

English works beautifully, and the underlying model supports 90+ languages with auto-detection. Mixed Hindi–English speech is handled surprisingly well, and improving it further is on the roadmap.

What are the system requirements?

A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, or M4) and about 4 GB of free disk space for the app and speech model. Intel Macs and Windows aren't supported in this version.

Is Windows coming?

It's planned. The design ports cleanly; the speech engine just needs a different backend on Windows. Mac first, done well, then Windows.

What does "Full" cleanup mode need?

The free Ollama app running a small local language model. It's optional — Hush works out of the box in Light mode — and like everything else, it runs entirely on your machine.

How is it free? What's the catch?

There isn't one. Hush has no servers to pay for because your Mac does the work. The code is a few hundred readable lines of Python — you can open it and see exactly what it does, which is a promise no closed cloud app can make.

Your voice deserves privacy.

Free, offline, and quietly yours. Setup takes about 15 minutes.
