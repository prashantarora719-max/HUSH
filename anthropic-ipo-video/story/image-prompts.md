# Images for the full story-mode film (22)

Attach the neutral Frank reference image to every Frank prompt. Download each at full size, 16:9. Reject any image with lettering or logos in it.

**Style line for Frank poses (1 to 14), paste first:**
`Same man, same clothes and same illustration style as the reference image. Editorial ink-and-wash illustration, black ink linework, soft watercolor shading, charcoal, cream and burnt-orange palette, plain flat cream background (#f0eee6), no text, no logos, 16:9 widescreen, high resolution. Waist-up, centered. Nothing else in the frame.`

Then add one of these:

1. **Small print:** Holding a sheet of paper close to his face, squinting at it, glasses still pushed up on his forehead, one finger raised as if making a point.
2. **Not so bad:** Relaxed half-smile, one hand making a "that's reasonable" open-palm gesture.
3. **That's worse:** Wincing, teeth clenched, shoulders hunched, eyes squeezed half shut.
4. **Which one is it:** Pointing straight at the viewer with one finger, one eyebrow raised, skeptical.
5. **Deadpan:** Completely flat expression, staring straight at the viewer, arms hanging at his sides, unimpressed.
6. **By doing well:** One hand covering half his face in a facepalm, one eye peeking through his fingers.
7. **So it's safe:** Hopeful smile, giving a thumbs-up with one hand, eyebrows raised.
8. **On purpose:** Head tilted back, looking up at the ceiling, both hands thrown up in exasperation.
9. **Wait:** Leaning forward, one index finger raised, eyes narrowed, mouth open mid-objection.
10. **Hold on:** Suspicious squint, chin lowered, one hand stroking his stubble.
11. **Boat:** Sheepish grin, holding a small glossy brochure with a picture of a fishing boat on it, glancing sideways.
12. **Needs his glasses:** Patting his shirt pocket with one hand and his trouser pocket with the other, looking down, puzzled, while his reading glasses sit clearly visible pushed up on his forehead.
13. **Glasses on, reading:** Reading glasses now on his nose, holding a printed statement in both hands, eyebrows raised in realisation.
14. **Last word:** Arms crossed, small knowing half-smile, looking straight at the viewer.

**Style line for scenes (15 to 22), paste first:**

18. **The circle:** Five business people in suits standing in a ring, each handing a thick stack of banknotes to the person on their right, seen from above at an angle, everyone smiling.

21. **Forced buyers:** A dozen men and women in dark suits squeezing shoulder to shoulder through one narrow doorway at the same time, briefcases held overhead, wide shot, comic crush.
22. **The statement:** Close view over the shoulder of the same man from the reference image at his kitchen table, reading glasses on, his finger pointing at one highlighted line on a printed account statement whose writing is only blurred grey lines.
