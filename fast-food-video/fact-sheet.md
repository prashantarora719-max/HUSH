## The story this supports
1. Frank's burger costs far more than it did, and prices rose faster than inflation.
2. Costs did go up (beef, wages), so it is not all greed.
3. The twist: the company at the top keeps about 40 cents of every dollar it takes in, because it mostly collects rent and royalties while the franchisee carries the costs and sets the price.
4. The customers who left are the ones the dollar menu was built for.
5. What to watch: value meals, and whether prices come down.

## Do not use
- A site claiming McDonald's margins fell to 8 to 12%, a federal $18.50 wage floor and an FTC delivery-fee cap: unverified and contradicted by the company's own figures.
- Single-store prices (such as a $7.59 Big Mac at one location) as if they were national.
