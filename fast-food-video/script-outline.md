## Cold open (0:00 to 1:00), fully written

*Counter. Menu prices tick upward as Frank walks in.*

**CASHIER (off screen):** Eleven dollars.
**FRANK:** For a burger, fries and a drink?
**DANNY:** *(not looking up from his phone)* That's what they cost, Grandpa.
**FRANK:** In my day this was a dollar.
**NARRATOR:** It wasn't. But he has a point. McDonald's says its own menu prices rose about forty percent in five years. That is faster than almost everything else you buy.
**FRANK:** So where's it going?
**DANNY:** Beef. Wages. Everything's expensive.
*A man in a suit walks behind the counter. The manager hands him an envelope. He leaves.*
**FRANK:** ...Who's that guy?
**NARRATOR:** Good question, Frank. Somebody is taking your eleven dollars. We have four suspects. And the one who gets the biggest slice has never cooked a burger in his life.
*Suspect board: four silhouettes. Title card.*

## Running jokes
- Frank writes every clue on a napkin. The napkin gets fuller each scene.
- Danny never looks up from his phone until the reveal.
- The Landlord never speaks until the last line.

## Fairness notes
- The Landlord stands for the corporation, not a real person. No real executives are drawn.
- The franchise owner is shown sympathetically. The video does not claim anything illegal: this is the business model, described in the company's own filings.
- No logos, mascots or brand artwork. The name appears in text only.
