# Illustration prompts (for Grok)

Nine illustrations that give the video a human, hand-made texture between the charts. Generate each at 16:9 and upload the ones you like; I'll animate them with slow camera moves, layered parallax and crossfades.

**Paste this style line at the end of every prompt so they all match the thumbnail:**

```text
Bold illustrated editorial style, thick black outlines, cel-shaded, clearly a drawing and not a photograph. Limited palette: charcoal black, deep red, bright lime green accents, off-white. Dramatic lighting. 16:9 widescreen. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.
```

## Notes

- **No real faces except Huang.** Only prompt 1 shows a real person, from behind. The other scenes use metaphor, which avoids rights problems and reads as more cinematic.
- **Keep them drawn.** If any come out looking like photographs, add "cartoon, not photorealistic" and regenerate.
- **Leave space.** Wide shots with dark empty areas work best, because I overlay numbers and captions on them.
- **You don't need all nine.** Prompts 1, 2, 5, 8 and 9 do the most work. The rest can stay as motion graphics.
