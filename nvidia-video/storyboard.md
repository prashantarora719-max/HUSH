# Can Nvidia Fall? Storyboard

Shot-by-shot plan for the full video. Timings are estimates at a slow documentary pace with pauses (about 18:45 in total; a brisk read would be nearer 15:00) and will be re-cut to the recorded voiceover.

## Visual language

- **Canvas:** 1920x1080, 30 fps. Near-black background with a faint drifting grid and a vignette.
- **Colour means something:** green is growth and strength, red is damage and risk, amber is a warning, grey is context. Never use red or green decoratively.
- **Type:** Inter Display (heavy) for numbers and statements; JetBrains Mono (spaced capitals) for labels, dates and sources.
- **Motion:** numbers count up or down rather than appear; charts draw themselves; hard cuts and a short shake on bad news; slow push-ins on quotes.
- **Running device:** the chip with five cracks. Each chapter opens by returning to the chip and lighting up its crack, so the viewer always knows where they are.
- **No logos, no photos of real people.** People are shown as silhouettes or name cards. This avoids rights problems and keeps the style consistent.
- **Source tags:** every statistic gets a small mono source line in the bottom-left for two seconds.

## Production notes

- **Total:** 67 shots, about 18:45.
- **Sound (for your editor):** low pulse under the cold open; a hit on shots 3 and 4; silence under the two quote cards; one recurring motif each time the chip returns.
- **What I can animate directly:** every shot above is charts, type, diagrams or simple line art, so all of it can be built the same way as the pilot.
- **What would lift it further:** licensed archive footage or photos for shots 10 to 14 and 55, if you want a human texture alongside the graphics. That is an editor's job and a rights question.
