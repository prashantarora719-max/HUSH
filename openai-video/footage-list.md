# Footage list: OpenAI video, cold-open test

Eight clips for the first 40 seconds. Once the test looks right, I'll send the list for the rest of the video.

**Where:** pexels.com/videos (free, and its licence allows commercial use on YouTube without credit).
**What to pick:** landscape, 1080p or higher, 8 to 20 seconds, steady or slowly moving camera, no company logos, no recognisable faces in close-up.
**How to send:** attach them in the chat, or upload them to the `openai-video/footage/` folder of the GitHub repo. Name them 01 to 08 so I know which is which.

| # | For the line | Search for | What I want to see |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | "This company is worth one and a quarter trillion dollars." | `city skyline night aerial` | Glass towers at night, slow drone move. Money and scale. |
| 02 | "On paper." | `signing document close up` or `stack of papers` | A hand and paper, shallow focus. |
